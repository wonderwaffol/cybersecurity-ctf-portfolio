# Cybersecurity CTF Portfolio

This repository documents hands-on Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges and independent cybersecurity exercises I completed outside my university coursework.

My write-ups focus on the investigation process: what I observed, why I chose each next step, what the evidence showed, and how the work relates to real security responsibilities. I aim to describe my experience accurately, distinguish guided work from independent discoveries, and avoid overstating my role or skills.

## Areas of Interest

- Security Operations (SOC)
- Incident Response and Digital Forensics
- Vulnerability Management
- Identity and Access Management (IAM)
- Threat Intelligence

## How These Write-ups Are Prepared

Each entry is reconstructed from available evidence such as notes, screenshots, terminal commands, challenge files, and contemporaneous conversation records. Before publication, I review the material for:

- event and platform disclosure rules;
- flags, answers, private infrastructure, and challenge URLs;
- credentials, tokens, cookies, session identifiers, API keys, and other secrets;
- personal or identifying information;
- copyrighted challenge material that should not be redistributed; and
- technical claims that are unsupported by the available evidence.

Unknown details are labelled as unknown or omitted rather than inferred. A completed challenge is not automatically considered safe to publish.

## Scope

This portfolio currently excludes university assignments and school projects. It is intentionally selective: screenshots, commands, badges, and files are included only when they provide useful evidence or clarify the technical reasoning.

## Ethical Use

All documented activity was performed in authorized CTF or training environments. The material is shared for educational and professional portfolio purposes. Sensitive information and restricted challenge content are excluded.
